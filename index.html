Progress through feedback.

Reinforcement learning trains an agent through interactions with an environment. The goal is to learn a policy that performs better under a defined reward system.

03 / THE MOTIVATION

When access is difficult,
every decision matters.

Natural disasters can make search and assessment difficult. Robotics research explores tools that may help responders gather information in places that are dangerous or hard to reach.
