OUR MISSION

Better experiments.
More thoughtful autonomy.

We want to make it easier to explore how autonomous agents learn, how their performance can be evaluated, and where experimental results have limitations.

Our long-term vision is a modular research platform that can connect configurable environments, reproducible training, and transparent evaluation.
