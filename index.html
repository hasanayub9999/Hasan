OUR MISSION

Teaching drones to navigate better.

SkyRL is an experimental research project exploring how reinforcement learning can help autonomous agents learn navigation policies in simulated environments.

Experimentation

Change one setting, run it, and look at what happened.

Reproducibility

Record every configuration and seed so results can be repeated.

Evaluation

Compare runs on shared metrics, with clear labels on what is simulated.

SkyRL is not a certified real-world flight system. Simulation results do not prove real-world safety or readiness.
