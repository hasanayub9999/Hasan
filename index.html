# Initializing SkyRL Environment Simulation
import skyrl

env = skyrl.make("UrbanCanyon-v2")
agent = skyrl.PPOAgent(policy="MLPPolicy", lr=3e-4)

for episode in range(500):
    obs = env.reset()
    done, total_reward = False, 0.0
    while not done:
        action = agent.predict(obs)
        obs, reward, done, info = env.step(action)
        total_reward += reward

Platform Capabilities

Designed for systematic experimentation, reproducibility, and rigorous reinforcement learning research.

Environment Design

Configure experimental navigation spaces with customizable obstacles, wind vectors, sensor noise profiles, and target waypoints.

Policy Training

Explore how reinforcement learning policies (PPO, Actor-Critic) improve over iterative simulation episodes and reward shaping.

Evaluation

Compare experimental results, tracking convergence rates, success ratios, episode lengths, and collision frequencies.

Community Research

Built with modular Python abstractions, making the platform extensible for collaborative student and researcher experiments.

Note on Scope: SkyRL is an experimental simulation platform. Capabilities reflect implemented reinforcement learning loops and Gym-compatible benchmarking tools within the workspace portal.

Research & Real-World Context

Examining why autonomous navigation and disaster-response technology remain critical areas of scientific study.

Complex structural inspections and rapid post-disaster response scenarios present severe operational hurdles. When natural disasters strike—such as the magnitude 7.1 earthquake that impacted Puebla, Mexico on September 19, 2017—first responders face unstable terrain, blocked access routes, and damaged infrastructure that endanger human survey teams.

These high-stakes environments motivate ongoing academic and industrial research into autonomous robotic navigation, robust sensor fusion, and reinforcement learning control policies. While simulation environments cannot replicate every nuance of physical reality, they provide safe, reproducible testbeds for algorithmic exploration.

Our Mission & Vision

Advancing accessible, reproducible reinforcement learning experimentation for autonomous systems.

SkyRL was created for HackPHS 2026 with a clear objective: to build a rigorous, transparent platform where students and researchers can study how reinforcement learning agents learn spatial navigation policies. We believe that open experimentation, clear performance metrics, and rigorous evaluation are essential for advancing autonomous system research.

Important Safety & Scientific Disclaimer: SkyRL is an experimental research project, not a certified real-world flight control system. Simulation metrics and demo training runs are intended solely for educational evaluation and algorithmic exploration. Simulation success does not guarantee real-world safety or operational readiness.
