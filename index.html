HOW IT WORKS

A practical learning loop.

Training is iterative. Agents interact with an environment, learn from feedback, and are evaluated against a defined task.

01

Design

Define the environment and the objective.

02

Interact

Let the agent take actions and observe outcomes.

03

Train

Use rewards and experience to update the policy.

04

Evaluate

Measure performance and identify areas to improve.

THE HUMAN CONTEXT

In disaster response,
every second matters.

Earthquakes can damage buildings, block access and leave people trapped beneath debris. Search-and-rescue teams work in difficult, unpredictable conditions. Technology that helps responders assess an environment could be valuable when properly tested.

WHY THIS MATTERS

These examples show the challenges of disaster response; they do not establish that drone limitations caused the reported deaths. SkyRL investigates reinforcement learning in simulation as a potential research direction. It is not a proven rescue system, and simulation alone cannot establish real-world safety.

THE LONG-TERM VISION

Better experiments.
More capable agents.

SkyRL aims to make it easier to create navigation challenges, examine agent behavior and compare training results. Over time, shared experiments could help researchers investigate how policies perform across different environments.
